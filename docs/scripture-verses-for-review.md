# Scripture for prayer texts: draft for review

Berean Standard Bible (BSB), public domain. Every passage is copied word for word from the BSB's own text file. Psalm headings ("A Psalm of David") are left off, and quotation marks are closed where a passage stops mid-speech.

When someone adds a verse to an "I prayed for you" text, they pick a theme and the app offers these passages in turn ("Different verse" moves to the next).

**To change the list:** strike a passage, suggest a replacement reference, or move one to another theme. The list lives in `lib/models/scripture_verses.dart`.

- **Isaiah 26:3**: You will keep in perfect peace the steadfast of mind, because he trusts in You.
- **Numbers 6:24–26**: ‘May the LORD bless you and keep you; may the LORD cause His face to shine upon you and be gracious to you; may the LORD lift up His countenance toward you and give you peace.’
